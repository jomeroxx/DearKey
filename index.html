Does it work with your phone?

Most modern phones support NFC.

iPhone

iPhone XS and newer with iOS 17+ — just tap. Older models may need one extra step.

Android

Most recent phones work. Turn on NFC in Settings → Connected devices.

How does it work?
Tap the keychain on the back of your phone. Your message, song, and photos appear automatically on most phones.
What can I customize?
A song or playlist, a personal message, 1–5 photos, and keychain style.
How long can the message be?
